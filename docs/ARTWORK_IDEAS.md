# Artwork Ideas

Use artwork to make each management screen feel like a place or workbench, not a decorative poster. Prefer images with clear subject matter and enough calm space for the real UI to sit beside them.

## Current Application Artwork Slots

- Dashboard: party entering a dungeon, campaign table, or lantern-lit map desk.
- Character Management: hero roster wall, gear table, or portrait studio.
- Troupe Management: travelling camp, caravan ledger, or adventurers gathering around a route map.
- Guild Management: guild hall, contract board, coffers, or oath table.
- Party Management: four adventurers preparing at a table, campfire planning, or party banner.
- Equipment Shop: shop counter, weapons rack, armour stand, or merchant stall.
- Banking and Finance: guild banker, vault desk, coin scales, sealed ledgers, hidden strongbox.
- Settlement Management: friendly town gate, market square, inn exterior, or service district.
- Campaign Management: world map table, hex map, settlement pins, chronicle journal.
- Adventure Management: stacked modules, parchment maps, wax seals, dungeon sketches, adventure folios.
- Go Adventure: camp at the dungeon mouth, party leaving settlement, torchlit entrance.
- Rules Reference: open rulebook, bookmarks, annotation desk, organized library.
- Tables List: dice, reference cards, indexed table book, sorted scrolls.
- Credits / History / Background: shelf of owned books, archive desk, history wall.
- Game Guides: illustrated manual, checklist board, mentor table.
- Developer Section: blueprint desk, tool chest, map calibration table, asset inspection board.

## Additional Places Worth Adding Artwork

- Character sheet portrait strip: one portrait per character, shown in roster rows and full sheets.
- Class dossier backgrounds: subtle class-specific art behind the class preview, not behind controls.
- Adventure module cards: cover art per module, separate from the page-level Adventure Management image.
- Generated Adventures Guild leads: lead-family art for Rumor, Treasure Map, Thematic Dungeon, and Guild Job.
- Exploration objective banner: small scene/objective artwork when a supported objective is active.
- Settlement records: optional image per friendly settlement and later per troublesome town.
- Campaign map panel: hex-map texture or campaign-world parchment background once the map feature grows.
- Guild job cards: contract-board thumbnails keyed by job type.
- Treasure/reward closeout: small chest, relic, or reward banner art when a reward is ready.
- Banking ledgers: small account/vault icons for bank, trove, robbed account, inheritance, and loan states.

## Layout Guidance

- Keep text and controls off detailed artwork unless the image is deliberately dark and quiet.
- Use large artwork as a companion to status/command panels rather than as a full-page background.
- Use small artwork for repeated records only when it helps scanning, such as module cover art or character portraits.
- Avoid mock UI inside artwork; it competes with the actual app.
- Keep PDF crops local unless publication rights are secured.
